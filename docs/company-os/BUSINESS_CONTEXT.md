## Objective and constraints

Build a portfolio of useful digital solutions to problems buyers pay to solve, generating increasingly automated second income alongside full-time employment. Remain research-led, niche-flexible, internationally sellable, product-led, low-overhead and low-maintenance. Keep the business separate from employer/client/agency activity. Neither the owner's profession nor the current microstock module selects the best niche by default.

Primary KPI: **net profit per total hour invested**. Count research, validation, production, QA, packaging/listing, support, monitoring and maintenance; build/support hours alone are incomplete. Unknown revenue, expenses, demand, integration status and time remain unknown, not zero. Targets and forecasts are plans, not verified results.

Preserve the Charter's 5–7 founder hours/week and six-month planning ceiling; neither authorizes spend. Free cloud operation without credit-card signup remains the infrastructure preference. Reuse Cloudflare, GitHub and existing integrations; don't restart abandoned Oracle/n8n/Render setup proposals. New paid services, subscriptions, ads, authority changes, publication and production deployments need scoped approval.

## Commercial decision contract

Validate the specific buyer, problem, existing spend, close competitors, differentiated outcome, supported price, smallest sellable version and expansion path before major production. Use fresh permitted research for market questions. Cite dates and sources; distinguish observed facts, qualitative proxies, hypotheses and model inferences. Publisher guidance, asking prices, catalog size and discussion volume do not establish purchases.

Exact opportunity weights:

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Demand | 20 |
| Willingness to pay | 20 |
| Competition / market gap | 15 |
| Problem intensity | 15 |
| Differentiation | 10 |
| Build speed | 10 |
| Low maintenance / automation | 5 |
| Upsell / expansion | 5 |

80–100: strong build candidate; 70–79: validate first; 60–69: watchlist; below 60: ignore unless a strategic reason is recorded. Never invent missing ratings. A score does not authorize spending or production. Draft PR #7 additionally requires paid-performance and competitor evidence before computing a full score; that stricter gate remains a draft design, not an amendment silently applied to the master.

Prefer short MVPs, reusable IP, useful calculations/data/structure, strong UX and low support. Every product needs a reason to buy rather than generate it with AI. Do not automatically adopt or reject generic templates, art, planners or prompt packs; require an evidence-backed advantage. Choose sales channels by fit, discovery, fees, payments, customer ownership and economics, not a default platform.

Portfolio planning mix: 60% evergreen / 25% growth / 15% experimental, not production quotas. Validate → small product → product family/bundle → automation/recurring revenue → software only where justified. Exploit measured winners before endless novelty. Test positioning, price, packaging and listings before retiring weak products; avoid indefinite repair of unwanted products.

Pipeline: IDEA → RESEARCH → VALIDATION → APPROVED → MVP → TESTING → LAUNCHED → OPTIMIZING → SCALING → MAINTENANCE → RETIRED. Monthly cadence: discover, validate, build, sell/optimize. Optimizing an existing winner can replace a new launch. Quarterly reviews: START / CONTINUE / SCALE / STOP.

No organic sales means no ad spend by default. Five to ten credible organic sales may justify proposing a small test, not executing it without approval. Scaling needs understood acquisition cost, positive contribution margin, acceptable refunds and measured conversion. Forecasts require downside/conservative/base/strong scenarios with explicit units, price, fees, costs and time assumptions.

## Existing asset module

Microstock is an existing test vertical, not a validated strategy for the full business. For visual assets, favor recognizable reusable modular objects with consistent style and practical compositing utility. Historical 4000×4000/3D-only instructions applied to particular batches, not all future products. Record provenance per asset: original modeled renders and AI-generated batches have different marketplace eligibility.

Inventory existing assets and prior submissions before reuse. Preserve originals and encrypted receipts. Preparation, transfer, portal submission, review, acceptance, publication and sale are distinct states. No invented Contributor API or automatic final-submission capability. Use supported owner-authorized exports for sales data when direct integrations are unavailable. Private exports, financial records, assets and credentials stay outside this public repository.
