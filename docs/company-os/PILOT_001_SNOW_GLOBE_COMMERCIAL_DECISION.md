# Pilot commercial decision 001 — modular snow-globe 3D assets

**Decision date:** 2026-11-02  
**Status:** source-backed preliminary comparison, **not a confirmed profitable niche**.  
**Proposed verdict:** **VALIDATE** any existing usable assets; **do not authorize** another mass Christmas snow-globe batch from this evidence.

## Product and buyer hypothesis

**Potential buyer:** independent craft, card, POD, social-media and photo-mockup designers who want ready-to-compose transparent scene pieces or empty customizable snow-globe frames rather than a flat scene they cannot customize.

**Proposed differentiated small MVP (hypothesis):** 4–6 genuinely original, composable 3D empty snow-globe frames/background-ready objects with authentic alpha transparency, useful negative space, consistent angles, 4K dimensions, and one professional preview/composition guide. This is a **test concept**, not approved production. Actual file inventory and provenance have not yet been inspected.

## Publisher-led buyer-use signals, not sales proof

[Adobe Stock's Creative Components call](https://stock.adobe.com/pages/artisthub/get-inspired/design-assets-content-needs) explicitly discusses utility-style isolated PNGs, compositing elements, design overlays, mockups, celebrations, borders and useful design materials. This supports the **format/use case**, not the idea that *snow globes specifically* are profitable or undersupplied.

[Shutterstock's contributor guidance](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594691-what-type-of-content-are-shutterstock-customers-looking-for) distinguishes upcoming **seasonal** and **recent** customer search trends. This supports researching seasonal lead times, not making up keyword search volume.

## Time and portfolio

- November 2 is **later than** the existing seasonal research calendar's October 11 ideal stock upload date for Christmas 2026. That upload-by date was a **75-day planning heuristic**, not a formal platform cutoff.
- Recreating dozens of brand-new Christmas designs now risks high time cost relative to a short remaining discovery window. Existing finished assets may still justify a small authorized test if their rights and quality are verified.
- A more prudent choice might be reusable evergreen frames, a 2027 seasonal concept, or a different product format. **No option is selected yet without competitor and sales evidence.**

## Missing material commercial facts

1. User's actual snow-globe collection inventory, original vs AI-generated provenance and platform acceptance status.
2. Whether buyers are specifically paying for **empty customizable/compositable** snow-globe elements, rather than decorated printable PNG scenes.
3. Product-level sales/download velocity for any close competitor.
4. A sample actual production/QA/metadata time per reusable asset.
5. Actual channel commissions, royalties, net receipts and any sales history from the user's contributor accounts.
6. Quality comparison of at least three close **empty-frame** competitor assets, not simply thematic snow globes.

## CEO recommendation to the Chairman

**VALIDATE, not BUILD.** Do not initiate an additional 50-asset run based on old seasonal exposure and generic Shutterstock/Adobe articles.

If files are already finished and policy-compliant, inspect them, select a small group, and run an approved listing test with tracked listing dates and downstream earnings. If we have no proven sales evidence, the decision engine must show **score: unknown** and **profit/hour: unknown**, not a fabricated 85/100 or revenue projection.

**Next gate:** an actual authorized export of contributor sales/earnings plus sample production timing. This must be joined to the listed asset filenames to assess return on production effort and decide which family deserves scale.

This is a worked research case, not approval to purchase ads, modify listings, or upload stock automatically.
